# HR manager guide

Approving vacancies and shortlists, overseeing decisions and the dashboard. Read the
[Recruiter guide](RECRUITER_GUIDE.md) first — HR managers can do everything recruiters can.

## 1. Approve and publish vacancies
1. Vacancies waiting for you appear on the dashboard and under **Jobs** with *Pending approval*.
2. Check the criteria, weights, essentials and their justifications, and the questions.
3. **Approve** (or return with comments), then **Publish** now or schedule it. Pause or close early
   when needed; closing early abandons unfinished drafts and candidates are told.

## 2. Approve shortlists
1. Open the submitted shortlist. Every decision shows who made it, the reason, and what the AI had
   suggested at the time (overrides are highlighted).
2. **Approve**. Choose whether undecided applicants stay undecided. If your organisation uses dual
   control, the approver must be a different person from the proposer.
3. Statuses change at once, but **candidates are told only after the notification delay** (set in
   **Settings → Organisation policy**). Until then you can **Undo** — nobody is emailed.
4. You can also return the shortlist to the recruiter with comments.

## 3. Dashboard and oversight
- **Dashboard:** vacancies, applications by stage, time in stage, and AI suggestions shown
  separately from human decisions. Numbers reconcile with the applicants lists.
- **Override analytics:** how often reviewers disagree with AI suggestions per vacancy. A high rate
  (over 30 %) suggests the rubric needs attention.
- **Screening runs:** re-evaluate a vacancy after a rubric change (a reason is required; results are
  new versions).

## 4. Settings you own
In **Settings → Organisation policy** (with the org admin): blind review default, notification delay,
dual control, AI screening on/off, optional external language model (off by default), retention
period, privacy notice text.

**Switching AI screening off** sends every new application to manual review with no AI output.
Nothing is lost; switch it back on and start a screening run when you want AI help again.

## 5. Accountability
Every consequential change needs a human and a reason. The **Audit** page (settings) lists events and
exports them safely to CSV; **reconstruct** any decision as a PDF for a complaint or audit.
