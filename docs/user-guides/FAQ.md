# Frequently asked questions

## Candidates

**Does a computer decide whether I get the job?** No. Software suggests which evidence in your CV and
answers matches the vacancy's criteria and explains why; people at the employer make every decision.
See *How we use AI* on the website. You can also ask for a review without AI assistance when applying.

**Something in my CV was read wrongly.** Correct it on your application before submitting; corrections
are shown to the employer next to the original.

**Is a gap in my career a problem?** Gaps, formatting and missing keywords are never counted against
you automatically; missing information is treated as unknown, not as a "no".

**Will other employers see my CV?** No. Applying to one employer never shares your CV with another.
Talent pools are per employer and only if you opt in.

**How do I get my data or delete it?** Privacy → New request (export, correction, deletion). You must
confirm with your password; employers have 30 days to complete it.

**How do I stop SMS or WhatsApp messages?** Reply STOP, or switch them off in Settings.

## Employers

**Can we auto-reject applicants below a score?** No. There is no automatic cut-off; outcomes need a
person and a reason. Shortlists can require a second approver.

**Why is an application "manual review requested"?** The candidate asked for a review without AI
assistance, AI is switched off for your organisation, or your monthly AI allowance is used up. A person
reviews it as usual.

**What happens if we don't pay an invoice?** After a 7-day grace period your team can view but not
change data until payment; published vacancies stay open to candidates and nothing is deleted.

**Which candidate data do webhooks and API keys expose?** Only identifiers, references and statuses –
never names, contact details, documents or AI output.

**Who can see AI scores in interviews?** By default panelists don't see AI bands, so interviews are
assessed on their own (organisation policy).
