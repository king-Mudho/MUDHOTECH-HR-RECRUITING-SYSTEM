## Observations for calibration (Phase 10)

- Most gold *not suitable* CVs land in **review needed**, not lower match: their essentials are
  usually *absent* from the CV, and absence is unknown rather than a fail (principle 3). This is
  the intended conservative behaviour, but it makes *review needed* the largest band (95/135).
  Phase 10 calibration should check whether clarification suggestions resolve these quickly and
  whether HR wants a separate "essentials not evidenced" filter.
- No suitable candidate is routed to lower match; no not-suitable candidate is recommended.
