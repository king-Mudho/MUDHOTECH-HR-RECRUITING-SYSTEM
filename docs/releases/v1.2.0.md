# MUDHOTECH HR RECRUITING SYSTEM v1.2.0 — final release

**Status:** released 2026-11-20 to production at https://hr.agribizframework.com (shared
agribizframework VPS, ADR-033), deployed by the `Deploy (production)` workflow from the tag `v1.2.0`
after CI passed and the owner approved. Semantic skill matching is off on this server (no AVX2,
D-029); integrations run in sandbox mode and billing is not enforced until the owner switches them on.

## New since v1.0

### v1.1 — the whole hiring journey
- **Interviews:** fixed times or candidate self-booking from proposed slots, calendar files, reminders,
  rescheduling and cancellation with reasons, panel conflict warnings, no-show handling (never an
  automatic rejection).
- **Scorecards:** per-criterion ratings with evidence, submitted independently (others' scores hidden
  until you submit), consolidated view for HR; AI bands hidden from panelists by default.
- **Offers and hiring:** approval chain, PDF offer letter, electronic acceptance with typed name,
  expiry and withdrawal, openings counter, hire records, and a confirmed close-out that tells the
  remaining applicants only after a person approves the exact list.
- **Talent pools:** candidates opt in per employer for 12 months (reminder before it ends; leave or
  delete any time); HR searches consented members only and invites them to apply.
- **Reports and exports:** time to review/shortlist/hire, funnel conversion, sources, completion,
  CV processing quality, AI-human agreement, clarification response; CSV/XLSX/PDF exports with a blind
  option, 15-minute links and audit; alerts suggesting a rubric review when decisions often go against
  the AI band.
- **Subscriptions and billing:** 30-day trial, plans with limits that never block candidates, usage,
  monthly invoices in USD or ZWG with VAT fields, payment by EcoCash, OneMoney or card (Paynow) or bank
  transfer, reminders, grace period and read-only mode with immediate reactivation on payment.
- **Platform console:** tenants, users, feature flags, skills taxonomy curation and versioned
  publishing, dead letters, system health, plans and pricing.

### v1.2 — connecting to other systems
- Google and Microsoft 365 calendar sync for interview panels; staff single sign-on.
- Opt-in SMS and WhatsApp updates for candidates (reply STOP to opt out).
- Signed outbound webhooks, scoped read-only API keys and job feeds for job boards.
- Shona and Ndebele (machine drafts awaiting human translation) with a language switcher.

## Requires owner set-up before use
Paynow keys, Google/Microsoft OAuth apps, an SMS aggregator and WhatsApp Business templates
(`docs/integrations/`); until then these run in sandbox mode. See `docs/HANDOVER.md`.

## Upgrade notes
Database migrations run automatically on deploy. Existing organisations start a 30-day trial on first
use of billing features; demo organisations are on the Enterprise plan. **Before upgrading pilot
organisations**, decide their plan (e.g. Enterprise or an agreed paid plan) and set it on their
subscription in the Django admin (`/django-admin/` → Subscriptions), or deploy with `BILLING_ENFORCE=false` until billing is switched on deliberately - otherwise their
trial ends after 30 days and, if unpaid, they become read-only after the grace period. Set the new environment
variables listed in `.env.example` (billing, integrations).
