# MUDHOTECH HR RECRUITING SYSTEM v1.0.0 — pilot release

**Status:** release candidate. The tag `v1.0.0` is pushed only after every item of
`docs/deployment/GO_LIVE_CHECKLIST.md` is ticked (or an exception is accepted by the owner). Pushing the tag starts
**Deploy (production)**, which promotes the images already tested on staging and waits for the owner's approval.

## For pilot organisations

v1.0 is a complete applicant tracking system with explainable, human-controlled AI screening:

- **Vacancies** with versioned rubrics (weighted criteria, justified essentials) and screening questions; questions
  about protected characteristics are refused; approval and scheduled publishing; a public job board with search,
  company pages and feeds.
- **Applications** in five mobile-friendly steps with secure CV uploads (type checks, antivirus, quarantine),
  receipts with a reference number, and a candidate portal with messages, to-dos and privacy controls.
- **Evidence-first AI screening:** CVs are parsed into claims linked to the exact page and line; every criterion shows
  its evidence or says *unknown*; bands are routing hints, never decisions; missing information is never a fail;
  protected attributes are never inferred or scored. AI can be switched off per organisation at any time.
- **Human decisions:** verify claims, ask candidates for clarification, compare applicants, blind review by default,
  shortlists proposed by recruiters and approved by HR managers (optional dual control), a notification delay with
  undo, reasons recorded for every consequential change.
- **Oversight:** dashboard, override analytics, an append-only audit log with CSV export, and decision reconstruction
  as PDF.
- **Privacy (CDPA / SI 155 of 2024):** candidates can export, correct or delete their data and object to automated
  processing; retention rules and legal holds; encryption of CV-derived text at rest; tenant isolation enforced in
  the application and in the database.

Getting started: the role guides in `docs/user-guides/`, the onboarding session (`docs/training/HR_ONBOARDING.md`)
and support (`docs/training/PILOT_SUPPORT.md`).

## Known limitations and follow-ups

- Legal review (D-08) must be completed before real candidate data is processed: POTRAZ registration/licensing,
  DPO, signed DPAs, breach-notification deadlines in the incident runbook.
- Frontend error reporting to Sentry is not wired yet (backend only).
- The external LLM provider is off by default and needs an owner decision and a DPA before use (D-06).
- Brotli compression is not enabled (the official nginx image has gzip only).
- Interviews, offers and hiring records arrive in Phase 12; talent pool and analytics in Phase 13; billing in
  Phase 14; integrations and localisation in Phase 15.

## Upgrade notes
